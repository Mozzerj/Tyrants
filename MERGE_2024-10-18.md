# 18 October 2024 Recovery Merge

This build uses the cleaned Tyrants foundation as the base and ports forward the meaningful gameplay changes found in `Tyrants.Altis` dated 18 October 2024.

## Recovered into the active mission

- Full three-branch research progression and research UI.
- Procurement research gates for armoured, heavy armour, rotary, artillery, fixed-wing, and autonomous vehicle categories.
- Infrastructure Factory Production research bonus.
- Strategic emplacement purchase flow.
- VLS emplacement, side-owned target lists, cruise-missile launches, and missile map marker.
- Emplacement map markers and JIP action reconstruction.
- Individual vehicle selection during campaign setup.
- Later starting-steel balance values.
- Later `mission.sqm` snapshot.

All gameplay-changing actions above were adapted to the server-authoritative foundation rather than copied with the original client-side `GameData` writes.

## Save-format changes

The active save key is `TYR_Save_v3`. Research, VLS targets, and emplacements are serialized explicitly. v2 foundation saves and the original legacy save key are migrated on a best-effort basis.

## Recovered but intentionally not activated

The 18/10 Counter-Battery Radar and Roadblock creation branches contained no implementation. Their research progression is retained, but their emplacement entries are labelled prototypes and cannot be purchased as finished features.

The experimental map-based research UI, early missile prototype, and `ResearchV2.sqf` are archived under `docs/prototypes/`. The empty `SquadsSetup.sqf`, duplicate legacy init scripts, legacy variable initializer, and duplicate vehicle-state helpers were not restored because the cleaned foundation already replaces them.

## Fixes made while porting

- Research costs are deducted only after prerequisite and affordability validation.
- Procurement mappings use the actual cleaned vehicle category indexes.
- Autonomous vehicles require Autonomous Capabilities rather than Fixed Wing.
- Emplacement creation, VLS target mutation, ammunition use, and launches are server validated.
- VLS targets and emplacements have defined persisted storage instead of ad-hoc array indexes/live objects.
- The original `sleep 1:` missile syntax error is removed.
- Timed VLS launch spacing runs in scheduled server code.
- Setup add/remove-all vehicle controls have unique IDs and corrected actions.
- Factory production research scales newly produced resources instead of multiplying stored stock.

## Validation boundary

Static validation passes, but this build has not been executed in the Arma 3 engine in this environment. Multiplayer locality, dialog behavior, projectile behavior, and persistence should be regression-tested in Arma before treating the merge as release-ready.
