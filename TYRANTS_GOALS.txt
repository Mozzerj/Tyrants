TYRANTS - GAME GOALS AND FEATURE ROADMAP
========================================
Development branch: v4 DEV

PURPOSE
-------
Tyrants is a persistent, large-scale Arma 3 strategic warfare campaign for two or three competing factions.

The campaign is not won simply by owning the most towns. The real objective is to find, disrupt and ultimately destroy the enemy command structure while protecting your own.

Territory, factories, resources, research, vehicles, intelligence and strategic weapons all exist to help a faction locate and destroy the opposing command networks.


CORE VICTORY GOAL - DESTROY THE ENEMY COMMAND NETWORK
-----------------------------------------------------
Each active faction has a physical command hierarchy in the world:

    MAIN COMMANDER
          |
    SENIOR / REGIONAL OFFICERS
          |
      FORCE COMMANDERS
          |
    SQUADS / VEHICLES / ASSETS

The Main Commander and command officers are real NPCs, not abstract menu values.

Human players are NOT automatically the strategic commander. Normal player deaths must not destroy the faction command structure.

Command personnel can be moved, protected, hidden, escorted, discovered and killed.

The enemy should not automatically know where command personnel are. Their location is learned through reconnaissance, sightings, intercepted communications, captured intelligence and research.

Command damage has progressive effects:

- Healthy command network: orders move quickly and the faction can coordinate several operations at once.
- Officer lost: orders for the affected forces take longer to arrive and may reroute through another officer.
- Several officers lost: reinforcement, retreat, assault, artillery, procurement and force reassignment orders become noticeably slower.
- Main Commander lost: the faction suffers a major temporary command disruption while a surviving senior officer attempts to assume command.
- Forces with no command route continue their last orders and local tactical behaviour, but receive new strategic orders slowly or not at all.
- Command personnel are slowly replaced if the faction still has a functioning command base and enough time/resources.
- Repeated kills faster than the replacement rate create a real opportunity to collapse the enemy network.

A faction enters COMMAND COLLAPSE when it has no functioning Main Commander, no valid command officers and no immediate replacement route.

Existing soldiers do not vanish during collapse. They continue local combat and defend themselves, but coordinated strategic control is effectively lost.

If command authority cannot be restored during the collapse period, the faction is eliminated from the campaign.

The campaign ends when only one hostile faction with a functioning command structure remains.

INTELLIGENCE AND RECONNAISSANCE
-------------------------------
HARD RULE: server truth is not faction knowledge. A faction only knows enemy information that one of its real intelligence sources actually collected.

[IN PROGRESS] Enemy commanders, officers, units, emplacements, factories and location ownership begin unknown unless actually observed. v4.0.10 adds persistent last-known contacts for enemy units, vehicles, aircraft, naval units and emplacements; physical command personnel now exist in v4.0.12; named hostile identification still requires future intelligence refinement.
[DONE-v4.0.10] Human players create intelligence through real visual observation. Cursor/normal-view and optical observations are sent to the server and validated for enemy faction, range and unobstructed line of sight before becoming faction knowledge.
[IN PROGRESS-v4.0.10] UAVs/drones only report what they can physically observe. Connected player UAVs and physical UAV entities now create line-of-sight contacts; more exact camera/sensor modelling can be refined later. When contact is lost, live tracking stops and the map keeps only the last observed position/time.
[PLANNED] Spies are assigned by the player to real map areas/routes/sites and only report what they can observe there; they do not reveal a whole region for free.
[PLANNED] Spy reports are timestamped observations such as vehicles arriving, troops present, officers seen, command vehicles, air-defence sites or convoys.
[PLANNED] Spies can be discovered, captured or killed.
[PLANNED] A later Intelligence Officer can automate spy tasking according to priorities chosen by the player (for example command personnel, air defence, factories or a geographic area).
[PLANNED] Satellites are tasked to a chosen map area and produce a snapshot during a real pass; they do not continuously reveal the enemy.
[PLANNED] Satellite research improves revisit time, scan area, resolution and identification quality, but information still becomes stale after the pass.
[PLANNED] Sensor detections and visual confirmation are distinct. Radar/counter-battery sensors can create a suspected contact or firing origin, but visual reconnaissance is required to confirm exact enemy presence/ownership where appropriate.
[IN PROGRESS-v4.0.10] Identification detail depends on what was actually observed: broad class -> exact type is implemented for physical contacts based on observation source/range. Named/high-value personnel identification comes with the command-network system.
[DONE-v4.0.9] Location/factory ownership is only updated for a faction when it is observed. A map can therefore show an old 'last observed owner' that may now be wrong.
[DONE-v4.0.10] Intelligence records use source + UTC observation time + last-known state rather than a magic percentage. Strategic locations and physical contacts both use this model, and contacts freeze when observation stops.
[PLANNED] Enemy radio/order activity may create a real SIGINT clue only when an appropriate friendly sensor/system can detect it.
[PLANNED] Captured personnel, command posts or physical intelligence can reveal specific information.
[DONE-v4.0.13] Strategic AI builds non-friendly location/contact decisions from the faction knowledge database, including stale/unknown information; it does not use hidden hostile truth to choose attacks or raids.
[PLANNED] VLS, artillery, air strikes and other strategic attacks may only use targets/coordinates legitimately known to that faction.
[IN PROGRESS-v4.0.10] Strategic map renders faction last-known contacts with age/staleness and identification state. Sensor-only/suspected contacts will be added with radar, counter-battery and SIGINT systems.

INDUSTRY, FACTORIES AND PRODUCTION
----------------------------------
[DONE] Server-authoritative economy/factory service foundation exists, but the old abstract resource-output model is NOT the target design.

[PLANNED] Factories produce physical war materiel, not abstract resource income.
[PLANNED] The primary manufacturing cost is production TIME and limited factory capacity, not an instant steel/ammo/fuel purchase price.
[PLANNED] Players choose exactly what each controlled factory is producing from its allowed production list.
[PLANNED] AI commanders use the same production queues and must choose what their factories manufacture.
[PLANNED] Vehicle factories queue selected vehicles; when production finishes, the completed vehicle becomes available at/near that factory.
[PLANNED] Munitions factories queue selected ammunition types/loads rather than generating generic 'ammo points'.
[PLANNED] Artillery shells, rockets, SAM missiles, C-RAM ammunition, VLS missiles and other specialist ammunition can require appropriate factory production.
[PLANNED] Factory specialisation can limit what a site can build (for example ground vehicles, heavy vehicles, aviation, general munitions or missiles).
[PLANNED] Production queues have visible item, progress, completion time and local stock for the owning faction.
[PLANNED] Capturing a factory changes real production capacity instead of simply increasing a resource-per-minute value.
[PLANNED] Destroying or disabling a factory interrupts its queue and reduces the faction's ability to replace losses.
[PLANNED] Infrastructure research can reduce build times, increase queue capacity, improve repair/retool speed or unlock advanced production.
[PLANNED] Retooling a factory to a different product may itself take time so production choices have strategic consequences.
[PLANNED] Vehicles and ammunition are finite. Replacing a lost tank, missile battery load or artillery stock should take production time.
[PLANNED] Logistics later moves completed ammunition/stock from production sites to the units and emplacements that need it rather than teleporting infinite ammunition everywhere.
[PLANNED] Manpower, fuel and other support constraints may exist where they add gameplay, but factories themselves are manufacturing sites rather than abstract resource generators.

DESIGN RULES
------------
1. The command-network war is the central game goal. New features should support it directly or indirectly.
2. Territory and economy create the means to win; they are not the win condition by themselves.
3. Information should matter. Players and AI should have to discover important enemy assets.
4. AI follows the same important rules as humans. Difficulty should not rely on cheating.
5. Important persistent records use IDs and data, not live Arma object references.
6. The server owns authoritative campaign state.
7. Clients receive only information they are entitled to know.
8. Losing one officer hurts; losing several quickly creates an opportunity; complete command destruction wins the war.
9. Existing troops should behave believably after command damage instead of instantly disappearing or becoming useless.
10. Performance matters: strategic systems should think in seconds, not every frame.
11. A feature is not considered complete until it works in multiplayer, survives save/reload and has sensible JIP behaviour.
12. Keep the development branch clean: no legacy GameData architecture, obsolete wrappers or dead prototype code in the active mission tree.
13. No omniscient enemy state: if a faction did not observe/detect it through a real source, that faction does not know it.
14. Losing visual/sensor contact freezes intelligence at its last-known state; the server must not keep updating enemy markers secretly.
15. Factories manufacture selected vehicles/ammunition over time; they are not passive abstract-resource generators.
16. Strategic weapons, artillery and air defence use finite manufactured ammunition and real sensor/target information.
